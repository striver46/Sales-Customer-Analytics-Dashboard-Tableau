## 🎯 Project Overview

An **interactive two-page Tableau dashboard** that analyzes **$733K in sales, $93K in profit and 1,687 orders**. The **Sales Dashboard** tracks revenue, profit and quantity trends against the previous year, while the **Customer Dashboard** looks at who is buying, how often, and who drives the most profit.

**Business questions the dashboard answers:**

- 💰 How are sales and profit growing compared with the previous year?
- 📦 Which subcategories earn profit, and which ones lose money?
- 📅 What do weekly sales and profit trends look like against the average?
- 👥 How many customers are repeat buyers?
- 🏆 How much profit comes from the top 10 customers?

## ✨ Dashboard Features

| Feature | Description |
|:--|:--|
| 📊 **KPI Cards with Trend Lines** | Current year vs. previous year, with highest and lowest month highlighted |
| 📦 **Subcategory Analysis** | Sales vs. prior year plus profit/loss by subcategory |
| 📅 **Weekly Trends** | Sales and profit against average reference lines |
| 👥 **Customer Analytics** | Customers, sales per customer, orders and order distribution |
| 🏆 **Top 10 Customers** | Ranked by profit with sales and order count |
| 🎛️ **Interactive Navigation** | Icon buttons to switch pages and open filters |
